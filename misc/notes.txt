English word list
=================
Downloaded from http://www.gwicks.net/dictionaries.htm, word list
with 194.000 words.

Command to split the list into words of different lengths:

for c in $(seq -f "%02g" 3 12); do grep -xE "[a-z]{$((10#$c))}" src/english3.txt | LC_ALL=C sort -u >src/english3-length-$c.txt; done

Only lines made entirely of the letters a-z are kept, so words with
apostrophes (didn't, entr'acte) are left out instead of being split
into fragments (didn, entr).
